# MarketForge AI 0.5.0 — Research Workbench and Prospective Benchmark v3

MarketForge AI 0.5 expands the local-first forecasting studio into a broader financial
research workbench.

## Highlights

- Public read-only candle imports for Binance Spot, Coinbase Exchange and Kraken Spot
- Exponential smoothing, momentum, mean-reversion and regime-aware forecasts
- Empirical and conformal-style uncertainty calibration
- Market-regime classification and volatility forecasting
- Scenario stress testing
- Multi-asset correlation and portfolio simulations
- Equal-weight, inverse-volatility, risk-parity and minimum-variance allocation
- Local projects, experiment records and model metadata registry
- Executive, research, risk and model-card report templates
- External paired benchmark-replication analysis
- Expanded backtest risk and performance diagnostics
- Desktop packaging scripts
- Accessibility and multilingual interface foundations
- Official Prospective Frozen Benchmark v3

## Benchmark Status

Prospective v3 is preregistered and incomplete by design. Its holdout begins on
1 August 2026 and ends on 31 October 2026. Collection remains blocked until
3 November 2026.

MarketForge currently makes no claim that its candidate is more accurate than Kronos.

## Important Limitations

- This is alpha research software.
- Public exchange APIs may be unavailable or revise recent data.
- Live connector requests were not part of the network-restricted package audit;
  provider parsers were tested against documented response structures.
- Optional Kronos weights are not bundled.
- Desktop executables must be built on and tested for each target platform.
- MarketForge does not provide financial advice or guaranteed results.
